Docusketch is hiring a
Remote AI ML Engineer
The fastest-growing European SaaS company is searching for an AI/ML Engineer!You can be our perfect match!About DocuSketch:DocuSketch, with its European Headquarters in Potsdam, Germany, is a leading European software technology provider for the digital real estate economy. With our product brands Immoviewer and DocuSketch, we have become one of the most successful and fastest-growing software vendors in the rapidly expanding property tech sector. Our enthusiastic customer base is located across Europe and North America. DocuSketch's groundbreaking digital technology greatly facilitates real estate companies and building restoration companies to digitize, visualize, and virtualize real estate properties. Our solutions help our clients realize tremendous cost-saving potentials, process optimization, and revenue growth. You’ll be working in a team that drives the company's AI development to develop algorithms for a variety of different use cases. This includes e.g. working with equirectangular, phone, or satellite images to detect doors, windows, or other objects inside of buildings or finding facets on the roof. You’ll be challenged by solving complex 3D geometry problems and working closely with our labeling team to ensure the best possible data quality.Responsibilities and Duties:· Research, train and deploy state-of-the-art machine learning models for object detection and 3D perception on equirectangular images· Quickly prototype new ideas/technologies to create proof of concept and demosQualifications:· Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science or equivalent experience· Excellent math skills· Experience with 3D computer vision algorithms· Preferably 5+ years of experience developing and training machine learning models· Strong Experience with Deep ML frameworks (PyTorch)· Strong Experience in Python· Self-motivated and energetic team player with a strong work ethic and cooperative attitude· Excellent communication and organizational skillsPlus:· Familiarity with building big data systems· Training very large models using multiple GPUs in the cloud· Experience with training machine learning models on equirectangular imagesWe are seeking a performance-driven, high-energy personality who has several years of experience in the field. Since you will work intensely with teams from many countries solid command of the English language is a prerequisite. We offer attractive compensation, unlimited career opportunities, and the possibility to work in one of the most exciting software companies in the industry. The position will be remote in any location of choice in Europe (outside Russia, Belarus, and Ukraine). Our headquarters are in Berlin, Germany, with major offices in Belgrade, Serbia, Lakeland, Florida, and in Canada.We look forward to receiving your application! Salary and compensation
